# Books

Ordered roughly from most practical and current to foundational. Publication years are for the editions listed.

## AI security

| Book | Author(s) | Publisher, year | Why read it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Practical AI Security** | Harriet Farlow | No Starch Press, 2026 | Hands-on attacking and defending of AI systems, with 30+ Python demos (adversarial patches, backdoors, poisoning, agent attacks, red teaming). |
| **AI Under Attack** | Kris Kimmerle, David Okeyode | Packt, 2026 | Covers prompt injection, memory and context abuse, RAG and agent exploitation, and red teaming methodology. |
| **AI-Native LLM Security** | Vaibhav Malik, Ken Huang, Ads Dawson | Packt, 2025 | Goes through each OWASP LLM Top 10 risk in depth. |
| **The Developer's Playbook for Large Language Model Security** | Steve Wilson | O'Reilly, 2024 | Written by the lead of the OWASP LLM Top 10. A clear, practical guide for builders. |
| **Adversarial AI Attacks, Mitigations, and Defense Strategies** | John Sotiropoulos | Packt, 2024 | Broad hands-on coverage: poisoning, evasion, extraction, privacy and LLM attacks. |
| **Generative AI Security: Theories and Practices** | Ken Huang et al. (eds.) | Springer, 2024 | Strategy, governance and the security architecture of GenAI. |
| **Not with a Bug, But with a Sticker** | Ram Shankar Siva Kumar, Hyrum Anderson | Wiley, 2023 | An accessible, story-driven introduction to adversarial ML by founders of AI red teaming at Microsoft and elsewhere. |
| **Adversarial Machine Learning** | Joseph, Nelson, Rubinstein, Tygar | Cambridge University Press, 2019 | The academic foundation. Theoretical. |
| **Machine Learning and Security** | Clarence Chio, David Freeman | O'Reilly, 2018 | ML applied to security problems (malware, anomaly detection), and how attackers evade it. Dated, but the ideas still hold. |

## Background: understanding LLMs and AI systems

| Book | Author(s) | Publisher, year | Why read it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Engineering** | Chip Huyen | O'Reilly, 2025 | How production LLM apps (RAG, agents, evals) are really built. Gives you the attack surface. |
| **Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)** | Sebastian Raschka | Manning, 2024 | Implement a GPT-style model step by step. Code: [rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch](https://github.com/rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch). |
| **Hands-On Large Language Models** | Jay Alammar, Maarten Grootendorst | O'Reilly, 2024 | A visual, practical introduction to how LLMs work and how they're used. |
